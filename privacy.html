CaptionPress – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

CaptionPress makes subtitles on your own PC. Your videos, audio and subtitle text are never uploaded to any server. Subtitle lines are saved only on your PC so you can continue later.

The first time you create subtitles, CaptionPress downloads the open-source Whisper speech-recognition model and the program that runs it from public servers (Hugging Face and jsDelivr). Only the model files are downloaded; nothing from your files is sent. After that the app works offline.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
