Choose a font

Any font installed on this PC can be used for the captions – in the preview and in the saved video. Pick one with Vietnamese letters (ă, ơ, ư, ệ…) for Vietnamese lyrics.

🎵 Tap timing

The song plays and you mark every line yourself – exact, the way Sayatoo does it. Hold Space (or the big button) while a line is sung: press when it starts, let go when it ends. Backspace or ← goes back one line to redo it, Esc stops. A slower speed makes it easier.
